What users do

After you save Google, each vault owner opens Storage and taps Connect Google Drive. Backups land in their own Drive, encrypted with their backup password.

FCM is optional. The app still polls while it is open. The service account only wakes the phone when a web login needs Allow.

reCAPTCHA and the login lockout only affect sign-in. Vault owners never see these keys or the attempt counters.

While the Google app is in Testing, add each family Gmail as a test user or Google will block them.
