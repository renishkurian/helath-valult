Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 30, 2026 · Last Updated: August 30, 2026
Strict Privacy Standards

1. Overview & Self-Hosted Architecture

Family Vault (accessible at https://bucket.rklab.online) is a private, self-hosted household management, health records, financial ledger, and digital archive platform designed solely for authorized family members and private personal use.

Because Family Vault is strictly self-hosted on private hardware, all your personal data, passwords, financial records, and medical archives reside exclusively on your private deployment. We do not operate multi-tenant commercial tracking servers, we do not sell your information, and we do not monetize user data.

2. Google User Data & OAuth Scopes

Family Vault allows authorized users to optionally integrate Google APIs for specific productivity features. We only request permissions necessary to provide these explicit functionalities:

  • Gmail Read-Only (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/gmail.readonly): Used solely by the Expense Analyser module to scan and import bank alerts, credit card transaction receipts, and digital account e-statements directly from authorized financial institutions.
  • Google Drive File Access (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file): Used solely to store and restore encrypted database backups created specifically by Family Vault in a dedicated folder named "Health Vault Backups".
  • Basic User Info (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/userinfo.email): Used solely to verify and display the authenticated Google account email inside your private settings page.

3. Google API Limited Use Disclosure

Family Vault's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

In accordance with the Google Limited Use Requirements:

  • No Third-Party Sharing: Google user data is processed locally on your private host and is never transferred, shared, or sold to external third parties or data brokers.
  • No Advertising: Google user data is never used or transferred for serving advertisements, retargeting, or personalized marketing.
  • No AI Model Training: Google user data is never used to train, retrain, or fine-tune generalized artificial intelligence (AI) or machine learning (ML) models.
  • No Human Reading: No human employees or external operators read your email messages or private files. Data parsing is fully automated and restricted to your private authenticated sessions.

4. Data Storage, Security & Encryption

All sensitive information, including API access tokens, personal passwords, and health documents, are encrypted at rest using industry-standard cryptography (AES-GCM, PBKDF2 / SHA-256 password hashing, and encrypted session cookies).

Network communication with Family Vault is strictly enforced over encrypted HTTPS/TLS protocols.

5. Data Retention, Revocation & Deletion

You retain full ownership and control over your data at all times:

  • You can disconnect your Google Account at any time via Expense Analyser → Settings → Disconnect Gmail.
  • Disconnecting immediately deletes the OAuth tokens and cached authentication credentials from the local database.
  • You can also revoke access directly from Google's security dashboard at Google Account Third-party Connections.
  • You can purge imported inbox emails or transaction records at any time using the in-app "Clear Inbox" and "Delete" tools.
